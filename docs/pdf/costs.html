The schedule

The order that makes sense, and roughly how long each takes.

Stage 1 — this week · ≈ £4/month

Make it legal and honest

  1. Register with the ICO (15 min, £52/yr). Legally required. Add the registration number to your privacy page.
  2. Enter your real prices in the Pricing tab and tick approve. Until then every enquiry needs you to send the figure.
  3. Decide your cancellation policy and payment terms. The assistant currently refuses to state either, because you have not set them.
  4. Replace the placeholder testimonials with real reviews you have permission to publish.

Stage 2 — 10 minutes · ≈ £11/month

Make it conversational

  1. Create an Anthropic account, add ~£5 credit, create a key.
  2. Set a £15/month spending cap in their console.
  3. Add the key to Vercel and redeploy.
  4. Re-run the test suite — the AI path has not been exercised before.

Stage 3 — 30 minutes · ≈ £11/month

Make it communicate

  1. Resend — verify merrycleaners.co.uk by adding DNS records. The fiddliest step on this list.
  2. Turn on mock mode while you rehearse, then turn it off. With email connected, a test conversation can reach a real person.
  3. Five-minute follow-ups — a free cron-job.org schedule, or Vercel Pro’s scheduler.

Stage 4 — 30 minutes · per transaction

Take payment

Stripe business verification, then add the key. Test with their free test keys first. Until then bookings are created unpaid with a reminder to invoice by hand.

Stage 5 — launch day · ≈ £48/month

Go live properly

  1. Switch Vercel to Pro. Required once this is commercial use.
  2. Point merrycleaners.co.uk at the new site.
  3. Decide about the four services the old site lists and this one does not: post-construction, hospitality, Airbnb (currently folded into end of tenancy) and before/after party cleaning. Add them, or accept the narrower offer.
  4. Supabase Pro when losing the data would genuinely hurt — realistically month three or four.

Stage 6 — later · ≈ £95+/month

Expand

WhatsApp (Meta verification takes days, cost ≈ £0), error monitoring, a professional mailbox, shared-store rate limiting, and — only when staff need dashboard access — proper user accounts.

The honest recommendation

Start at Scenario A, £21 a month. It is compliant, durable and functional. Then add in this order:

  1. Anthropic key (≈ £7/month) — the biggest improvement to customer experience for the least money.
  2. Supabase Pro (£20/month) — once losing the data would hurt.
  3. WhatsApp (£0) — worth the verification time.
  4. Everything else only when a real need appears.

For context

An agency-built website, plus a booking system, plus a CRM would be a four-figure build and well over £100 a month in subscriptions — without the AI agent, the audit trail or the escalation handling.

What varies with usage

  • Anthropic — the only per-conversation cost. Capped by the limit you set.
  • Stripe — only on money you receive.
  • Vercel and Supabase overages — realistically unreachable at your volumes.
  • WhatsApp — £0 for the replies this system sends.

One-off costs: none. No setup fees on any service listed.
